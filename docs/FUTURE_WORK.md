# CivicLens AI — Post-Hackathon Roadmap & Future Horizons

CivicLens was created to revolutionize citizen-state engagement through transparent, grounded, and ethical agentic AI. This roadmap outlines the strategic milestones planned beyond the `1.0.0-hackathon` release.

---

## Horizon 1: Geographic & Multi-State Expansion
- **National & State Portals**: Expand the portal registry and official source library beyond Tamil Nadu to cover Central Government schemes (CPGRAMS, PM-KMY, National Portal of India) and key state portals (Karnataka Seva Sindhu, Kerala e-Services, Andhra Pradesh MeeSeva).
- **Automated Gazette Gazetteers**: Ingest daily district and state gazette releases with automatic classification and alert notifications for registered Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs).

---

## Horizon 2: Full Indic Language Coverage
- **Pan-Indian Linguistic Parity**: Extend the bilingual (English and Tamil) foundation to include **Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, and Bengali**.
- **Colloquial Dialect Support**: Enhance language detection to handle mixed colloquial phrasing (e.g. Tanglish, Hinglish) and regional rural vocabulary for agricultural and revenue schemes.

---

## Horizon 3: Low-Bandwidth & Voice-First Civic Access
- **WhatsApp & Telegram Citizen Bots**: Deploy the Central AI Agent as an official WhatsApp Business bot, allowing citizens to forward viral rumors for instant fact-checking or send photos of civic hazards via messaging.
- **Voice-Driven Multi-Modal Assistance**: Integrate speech-to-text and text-to-speech for elderly or illiterate citizens, providing audible step-by-step guidance in their mother tongue.

---

## Horizon 4: Verifiable Decentralized Provenance
- **Immutable Gazette Fingerprints**: Anchor official source document SHA-256 hashes to a public verification ledger, ensuring that retroactive changes to government circulars can be independently proven.
- **Civic Trust Consortium**: Partner with academic civic tech institutions, fact-checking networks, and legal aid clinics to collaboratively curate and verify official source libraries.
